{{NOMBRE_APP}} ("the application") is a personal, non-commercial tool operated by an individual (the "owner") to consolidate and analyse the owner's own bank transactions.

Who this applies to

The application is used solely by its owner to access the owner's own bank accounts and, where they have given their explicit consent, the accounts of members of the owner's household. It does not provide a service to third parties.

What data is accessed

  • Account details (account name, IBAN, currency).
  • Account balances.
  • Transaction data (date, amount, currency, description, counterparty, references).

Data is accessed read-only through a licensed open banking (PSD2) provider, with the owner's explicit consent granted at their bank. The application never initiates payments and cannot move funds.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to consolidate, categorise and analyse the owner's finances for the owner's private use, and is stored on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared or disclosed to any third party, and is not used for advertising or profiling.

Consent and revocation

Access is granted through the owner's bank under PSD2 and expires periodically (about every 90 days). The owner can revoke it at any time from their bank or from the open banking provider.
